Introduction

QUDx Himaya was born from a simple but urgent reality: families in Cote d'Ivoire and across Africa often face financial insecurity when tragedy strikes. Traditional insurance systems are complex, inaccessible, and rarely aligned with Islamic values. QUDx Himaya exists to change that - to protect people, not profits, and to build a Sharia-compliant, ethical assurance system that empowers communities and safeguards generational wealth.

Our Story

From the beginning, QUDx Himaya has been guided by Islamic principles such as Faraid, Takaful, and Tabarru. We replace informal, inconsistent practices with structured, transparent, and halal systems. Our brand, symbolized by the elephant - Cote d'Ivoire's national emblem - represents strength, protection, wisdom, and loyalty. We are deeply rooted in our community, and our mission is to bring dignity, peace of mind, and financial stability to families who need it most.
