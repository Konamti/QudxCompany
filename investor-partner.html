Investor & Partner Section for QUDx Himaya

An ethical assurance model for families, investors, partners, and communities.

Introduction

QUDx Himaya was born from a simple but urgent reality: families in Cote d'Ivoire and across Africa often face financial insecurity when tragedy strikes. Traditional insurance systems are complex, inaccessible, and rarely aligned with Islamic values. QUDx Himaya exists to change that - to protect people, not profits, and to build a Sharia-compliant, ethical assurance system that empowers communities and safeguards generational wealth.

Our Story

From the beginning, QUDx Himaya has been guided by Islamic principles such as Faraid, Takaful, and Tabarru. We replace informal, inconsistent practices with structured, transparent, and halal systems. Our brand, symbolized by the elephant - Cote d'Ivoire's national emblem - represents strength, protection, wisdom, and loyalty. We are deeply rooted in our community, and our mission is to bring dignity, peace of mind, and financial stability to families who need it most.

Why Invest

  • Market Gap: Millions of African Muslim families lack access to ethical insurance and inheritance protection.
  • Unique Value: QUDx Himaya is Sharia-compliant, transparent, and community-driven.
  • Scalability: Starting in Cote d'Ivoire, with potential to expand regionally and globally.
  • Global Reach: Diaspora Muslims abroad can contribute easily via PayPal and digital platforms.

Investor Returns

  • Financial Returns: A share of surplus funds generated after claims and operational costs, distributed transparently.
  • Social Impact: Direct involvement in protecting families, building generational wealth, and pioneering Islamic finance in Africa.
  • Visibility & Credibility: Recognition as founding partners on our website and in community campaigns.

How It Works

  1. Contribution Pool: Members contribute voluntarily into a shared protection fund.
  2. Claims & Services: Funds are used ethically to cover claims and inheritance protection.
  3. Profit Sharing: Surplus funds are shared with investors or reinvested into community projects.
  4. Transparency: Every transaction is logged, audited, and reviewed for Sharia compliance.

Assurance for Investors

  • Sharia Board Oversight: Ensures compliance with Islamic principles.
  • Audit & Reporting: Regular financial reports shared with investors.
  • Legal Framework: Contracts aligned with local law and Islamic finance standards.
  • Security: SSL-protected site, PayPal integration, encrypted records.
